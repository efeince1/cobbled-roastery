Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you roast the coffee in the shop?

Yes. The coffee is roasted in small batches on site, so the beans are fresh when they reach your cup or your bag.

Can I buy beans to take home?

Yes. A range of single-origin coffees is available, as well as a decaf and a selection box. You can buy them in the shop or on the official website.

Do you offer wholesale roasting for businesses?

Yes. Cafés and other businesses can order roasted coffee. Check the wholesale page on the official website for details.

Is there room to sit?

The shop is small, with limited seating and a window seat that looks out on Thistle Street. It can be a good idea to come at a quieter time if you want to stay a while.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards, contactless and Apple Pay are accepted.
